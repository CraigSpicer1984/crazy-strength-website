Open Access

Your gym, your session

Open Gym is exactly what it sounds like. Come in, train the way you want to train, and leave. No programme to follow unless you bring your own. No class structure. Just you, the equipment, and the space to work.

It suits people who know what they're doing and want access to a proper facility. It suits members who train with us in a coached programme and want extra sessions in between. It suits anyone who isn't ready to commit to a full programme but wants to start moving.

🏋️
Full Equipment Access
Barbells, racks, platforms, dumbbells, and everything else in The Den. All yours for the session.
👤
Coach Always Present
A Crazy Strength team member is on site for every session. You're not training alone in an empty gym.
📅
Sessions Every Day
Multiple slots daily, seven days a week. Early mornings, lunchtimes, evenings — there's always a session that fits.

Is it right for you?

Who trains Open Gym

Experienced lifters

You know how to train. You want access to proper equipment and space to do it, without a class structure around you.

Programme members

You're in a coached programme with us and want extra volume or recovery work between your scheduled sessions.

People getting started

You're not ready to commit to a programme yet, but you want to start. Open Gym gets you in the door without any pressure.
