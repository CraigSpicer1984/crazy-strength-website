Weightlifting Coaching Courses

The only qualifications recognised for the BWL Coach Licence Scheme — required to coach athletes at national competitions.

Level 1 Award in Coaching Weightlifting

The first step on the BWL coaching pathway. Covers the processes and principles of coaching, safety, session design and how to assist qualified coaches. Includes 12–16 hours of updated e-learning incorporating the new BWL Technical Model, plus a one-day practical at Crazy Strength. Assessment is now online, giving you more time to focus on practical application on the day.

Level 2 Certificate in Coaching Weightlifting

The next step for qualified Level 1 coaches. Provides the knowledge and skills to work as an independent Olympic Weightlifting coach. Holding a Level 2 also makes you eligible to apply for the BWL Coach Licence, required to coach athletes at national competitions. Combines advanced e-learning with a one-day practical at Crazy Strength.
