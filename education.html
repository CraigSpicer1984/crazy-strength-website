British Weightlifting

Weightlifting Coaching Courses

British Weightlifting’s official coaching pathway — the only qualifications recognised for the BWL Coach Licence Scheme, which is required to coach at national competitions.

British Weightlifting · Approved Venue
Level 1 Award in Coaching Weightlifting

The first step on the BWL coaching pathway. Designed for those new to coaching who have some knowledge of the sport. Covers the processes and principles of coaching, safety, session design and how to assist qualified coaches. Includes 12–16 hours of updated e-learning (incorporating the new BWL Technical Model) plus a one-day practical at Crazy Strength. Assessment is now online, giving you more time to focus on practical application on the day.

Level 2 Certificate in Coaching Weightlifting

The next step for qualified Level 1 coaches. Provides the knowledge and skills to work as an independent Olympic Weightlifting coach. Holding a Level 2 also makes you eligible to apply for the BWL Coach Licence, which is required to coach athletes at national competitions. Combines advanced e-learning with a one-day practical at Crazy Strength.

British Powerlifting

Powerlifting Coaching Courses

Official British Powerlifting coaching qualifications. Craig Spicer is the South West Course Tutor for British Powerlifting — bringing national-level coaching education directly to Amesbury.

British Powerlifting · South West Tutor: Craig Spicer
Level 1 Coaching Course

The British Powerlifting Level 1 coaching course covers the foundational principles of powerlifting coaching — technique, programming, competition preparation and athlete development. Suitable for anyone looking to start coaching squat, bench press and deadlift. No prior coaching experience required. Booking is through the British Powerlifting website.
