What Is Small Group PT?

Small Group PT sits between one-to-one coaching and a regular class. You get real coaching attention, a structured programme built around you, and the energy that comes from training with other people who are serious about getting better.

Groups are kept deliberately small — because the point is quality, not volume. You'll be coached on technique, pushed when you need pushing, and held accountable in the way that solo training rarely does.

Whether you're building strength from scratch, competing in weightlifting or powerlifting, or just done with wandering around the gym without a plan — this is built for people who want to train with intent.
