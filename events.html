Introduction to Weightlifting Course

Four weekly Saturday sessions, 09:00–10:00. Learn to perform the Olympic lifts safely in a structured environment. Complete beginners welcome. Graduates are invited to continue on the Crazy Strength programme. Contact us for the next available dates.

InBody Body Composition Scanning

Regular sessions — check socials for dates. Track your body composition with the InBody scanner, available through Silverback Nutrition at Crazy Strength. Accurate, fast and a great tool for monitoring progress alongside your training.
