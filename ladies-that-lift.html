Ladies That Lift

Strength training for women. Proper coaching, real results, and a room where you belong from day one.

Book a Free Taster Ask a Question

Strength Without the Noise

Ladies That Lift runs as its own group, in its own sessions. It is not a modified version of something else. The coaching, the programming, and the environment are built around how women train and progress.

Members range from complete beginners to experienced lifters. The level of the group does not dictate your programme — your programme is written for you.

What Every Session Looks Like

  • A warm-up with purpose, not filler
  • Main lifts coached in real time
  • Accessory work programmed to your needs
  • Progress tracked every session
  • No comparison, no ranking, no competition between members
  • Free introduction session before you commit
