BUILT FROM THE BARBELL UP

Crazy Strength was established by Craig Spicer — 30 years in strength sport, a decade as Head Coach for British Army Weightlifting and Army Powerlifting, and the person who built this place on one conviction: everyone has more in them than they think.

That is what #BeAGoodHuman means here. It is not a tagline. It is how we coach, how we run the facility, and the standard we hold ourselves to every day.

Meet the team
  • 1996 Craig picks up a barbell for the first time.
  • 2001 Qualifies as a strength and conditioning instructor.
  • 2009 Begins work as a civilian Physical Training Instructor with the British Army.
  • 2014 Appointed Head Coach for British Army Weightlifting and Army Powerlifting — a role held for a decade.
  • 2019 Crazy Strength opens in Amesbury.
  • 2025 Crazy Strength moves to its own facility at The Beacon Centre, Solstice Park.
