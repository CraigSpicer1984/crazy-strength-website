## The idea

> On this page, **the journey every member takes — from first taster to the platform** becomes **the sequence the page performs as you scroll**.

Competitor test: could a CrossFit box or a commercial gym say this? No — the specific path from coached taster to named competition (British Powerlifting, BWL) is what makes this theirs.

## The ritual (the real sequence)

1. **Show up** — Book a free taster. No experience needed. You'll be coached from day one.
2. **Get coached** — Every session has a coach. You learn proper technique from the start.
3. **Pick your path** — Powerlifting, Weightlifting, Ladies That Lift, Legends, Cubs, Open Gym.
4. **Get on the platform** — When you're ready, we prepare you to compete. Some never do — that's fine too.
